# Endless, freeform game loop with manual retrieval

The game loop has no end condition (no timer, shot limit, or lives) — the player shoots endlessly and Score resets only on relaunch or via the Restart button, never automatically. Shots can be taken from anywhere on the court rather than designated spots, scored 2 or 3 points by real-basketball Zone with no max range, and no combo bonus for a Streak. We considered timed rounds, fixed shot spots, and auto-feeding the ball back to the player after a Make (all common in arcade basketball games), but chose against all three: the goal is a simple, low-pressure "basketball hoop simulator" that balances realism against arcade feel, not a scored arcade cabinet loop. Manual Retrieval (walking to the ball, or pressing Reset as a shortcut) applies after every Shot, including Makes — this is a deliberate pacing choice, not an oversight, so a future contributor shouldn't "fix" it by auto-returning the ball after makes.
