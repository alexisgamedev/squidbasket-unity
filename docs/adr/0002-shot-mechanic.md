# Bullet-time shooting with an oscillating power bar and pure-physics outcome

Shooting is a distinct player state entered by holding LMB, with three deliberate mechanics that a future reader might otherwise assume were simplified or missed: (1) **Bullet Time** slows the whole scene for the duration of Shooting, rather than shooting happening at normal speed — chosen for the precision-aiming feel of the trope, at the cost of the aim-under-slowdown feel being an open, tunable question rather than settled; (2) the **Power Bar** continuously loops between a minimum and maximum instead of filling once and holding, and if the player holds through 2.5 loops without releasing, a **Shot Timeout** forcibly exits Shooting, fires no Shot, and loses the ball (Retrieval required) — a real risk/timing cost for over-holding, not a free do-over; (3) make/miss is determined purely by ball physics against the hoop, with no hidden accuracy or "sweet spot" system nudging borderline shots — consistent with the realism-vs-arcade balance set for the overall game loop (see `CONTEXT.md`). All three were chosen over simpler, more common alternatives (a fill-and-cap bar, an assisted-accuracy model, real-time-only aiming) for a distinct, more skill-expressive feel, and shouldn't be "simplified" back without revisiting this reasoning.
